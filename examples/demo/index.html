winbox-go

WinBox.js fully ported to Go, compiled to WebAssembly — github.com/0magnet/winbox-go

Drag titlebars, resize from all edges and corners, double-click a titlebar to maximize, minimize several windows to see the taskbar stacking. Every window on this page is managed by Go code.

Docking is the one thing WinBox.js does not have, and it is opt-in. A docked window pins to an edge and keeps a fixed thickness; drag its inner edge to resize it, drag its titlebar to pull it off. By default it RESERVES its strip, so maximize a window and it fills what is left, and minimized windows stack above a bottom dock rather than under it. Dock several at once — each claims space inside what the earlier ones left, so they meet at corners instead of overlapping.
